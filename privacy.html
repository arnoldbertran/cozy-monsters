Privacy Policy

This application is a personal tool used only by its owner to publish the owner's own videos to the owner's own YouTube channel, Instagram account and TikTok account, through the YouTube Data API, the Instagram API and the TikTok Content Posting API.

It does not collect, store or share personal data from any other person. The only data processed are the owner's own access tokens and basic account information (such as the account name), which are stored locally on the owner's computer and are never sold or shared with third parties.

The app uses YouTube API Services (Google Privacy Policy), the Meta platform (Meta Privacy Policy) and TikTok for Developers (TikTok Privacy Policy).

The owner can revoke the app's access at any time from each platform's account settings.
